DinorobongTuboDanceProject

Keep the dancers moving. Tap each note in its lane as it reaches the line, and the dancers follow the music for as long as the note lasts.

  • Lanes: ASD or ←↓→, or tap the pads.
  • Longer notes, longer dance: each note shows how far the dancers move. An eighth note is +1 photo, a quarter note +2, a half note +4, a whole note +8.
  • Miss one and the dance breaks. The stage turns red until your next hit, which puts the dancers right back in step with the music.
